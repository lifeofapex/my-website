KaziBridge

Type

Mobile and web app design

Date

2023

Role

Lead designer

Client

KaziBridge

KaziBridge seeks to bridge the gap between vocational school graduates and individuals/businesses seeking skilled services, facilitating smooth interactions, dependable transactions, and exceptional service quality.

Background

KaziBridge is a social impact startup based in Uganda. The platform connects skilled vocational graduates—like electricians, plumbers, and carpenters—with people and businesses who need their services.
I joined the team as the product designer to help bring this idea to life by designing a solution that is simple, functional, and trusted by both clients and tradespeople.

The Problem

In Uganda, a lot of young people graduate from vocational schools with good skills, but many of them struggle to find real jobs. On the other side, people and businesses often need quality service providers but don’t know where or how to find reliable ones. There’s a huge disconnect between the service providers and the people who need them.

Research and insights

* According to UN data, youth unemployment rose slightly from 6.54 % in 2021 to 6.58 % in 2022 Source: eprcug.org 🔗 .


* A 2019–2020 labour force study revealed ~30% of institutionally qualified youth couldn’t find jobs, with semi‑skilled graduates facing even greater challenges. Source: eprcug.org 🔗


* In 2020, over 90% of Ugandans worked in the informal sector—self-employed or subsistence—showing limited formal job opportunities. Source: ulandssekretariatet.dk 🔗 .

Key insights

* Most graduates don’t have a platform to promote their services.
* Clients want vetted and reliable service providers, but they usually go by word-of-mouth.
* Both sides are open to using tech if it’s easy and trustworthy.

User insights recap

* Many Victor (skilled graduate) and Grace (service-seeking client) users directly reflect actual gaps.
* Simple onboarding for graduates, trusted profiles for clients, and localized matching are vital.

Design goals

* Make onboarding and profile creation super easy for graduates
* Build trust in the platform by showing verified profiles
* Let clients post job requests quickly and match them with available providers.
* Keep the design clean, clear, and accessible even on low-end smartphones

Service provider module

Tailored for vocational workers, this module enables service providers to showcase their skills, receive job requests, track job progress, and manage their earnings. The experience is simplified to ensure accessibility—even for users with low digital literacy.

Service consumer module

This module was designed for individuals or businesses looking to hire skilled professionals. It allows users to easily browse service categories, view detailed provider profiles, post job requests, and manage ongoing or completed jobs—all in a few taps.

Admin module

The admin dashboard provides backend control for monitoring platform activity. Admins can verify new service providers, manage job requests, track revenue, and ensure the overall smooth operation of the platform.

Outcome

* Artisans loved how easy it was to set up their profiles and get noticed
* Customers found peace of mind knowing they were hiring verified professionals

Tools used

* Figma (for wireframes, UI and prototyping)
* Notion (for research tracking and documentation)
* Google forms (for initial user surveys)

What I learned

Working on KaziBridge was more than just a design task—it was an opportunity to solve real problems for a user group I couldn’t physically reach. Not being based in Uganda made research a bit challenging, but it also taught me how to dig deeper, ask the right questions remotely, and design with empathy and clarity.

This project helped me grow in user-centered thinking, especially when designing for communities I’m not directly part of. It reminded me that great design isn’t just about visuals—it’s about context, understanding, and creating real value for real people.

CloudClinic

Type

Mobile App Design

Date

2022

Role

UX/UI Designer
Brand design

Client

CloudClinic Ltd

Accessing timely medical care is often limited by location and time constraints. Cloud Clinic aims to revolutionize healthcare by offering a mobile app and website enabling users to book virtual appointments with doctors, facilitating convenient consultations from anywhere.

Problem Identification

Many people in Nigeria and across Africa face delays, long queues, or lack of access to qualified healthcare professionals. The idea behind CloudClinic was to bring healthcare closer through a mobile-first, user-friendly platform that supports scheduling, consultation, medical history, and follow-up—all in one place.

Background story

The traditional healthcare system often requires patients to schedule appointments days or weeks in advance, travel to a physical location, and wait in crowded waiting rooms before finally seeing a doctor. This process can be particularly challenging for individuals with busy schedules, mobility issues, or those living in remote areas with limited access to healthcare facilities.

Goals

* Allow users to easily book virtual appointments with medical doctors.
* Let users consult doctors from anywhere using their mobile devices.
* Make healthcare more accessible for people with busy lives or in remote areas.
* Improve how users experience and interact with digital healthcare services.

Research method

We chose online surveys for their ability to reach a diverse audience quickly and affordably, while offering flexibility, anonymity, and built-in tools for easy data analysis.

Data Analysis

The objective of this was to assess user satisfaction and preferences.
The survey was conducted with a sample of over 3000 participants, aged 18 - 65.

Outcome

CloudClinic launched successfully and is currently live and performing well. The platform has made it easier for users to access medical consultations, manage prescriptions, and stay informed through a digital-first approach. Collaborating with doctors and pharmacists helped ensure the features were practical, medically sound, and user-focused—contributing to the app’s real-world impact.

What I learned

Working on CloudClinic was a deeply rewarding experience. Collaborating with medical professionals gave me valuable insights into how digital tools can support real-world healthcare delivery. I learned how to translate complex medical processes into intuitive user experiences—while balancing trust, privacy, and usability. This project strengthened my ability to design solutions that are both human-centered and high-impact.

Cazuku

Type

UI Design

Date

2024

Role

Lead product designer

Client

Cazuku

Cazuku is a B2B, B2C eCommerce platform built to streamline trade between merchants, suppliers, and everyday shoppers. It aims to make bulk and wholesale buying easier across borders, enabling African merchants to access goods globally while simplifying order management and logistics.

Problem Identification

Traditional supply chains often leave merchants stuck with inconsistent pricing, unreliable suppliers, and scattered order processes. For suppliers, reaching the right merchants and managing bulk requests digitally was a challenge. Cazuku set out to bridge this gap with a structured, user-friendly platform that connects both sides seamlessly.

Project Goals

* Simplify cross-border buying and selling for African merchants.
* Enable suppliers to manage inventory, pricing, and order fulfillment easily.
* Centralize logistics tracking and communication within the platform.
* Provide an admin system for overseeing users, performance, and disputes.
* Design a clear, conversion-focused landing page for awareness and onboarding.

My Solution

Since this was a UI design task, my focus was on designing a unified platform that bridges the gap between suppliers and merchants—making bulk buying and selling across borders more seamless. I introduced intuitive dashboards for both user types, streamlined product discovery, and integrated real-time communication and logistics features. Every design decision was made to simplify the wholesale process from end to end, while building a system that fosters transparency, trust, and operational efficiency.

User Roles

* Merchants:
Business owners looking to buy goods—locally or internationally—in bulk, with better visibility on pricing, delivery timelines, and supplier credibility.

* Suppliers:
Product sellers seeking a platform to showcase their inventory, fulfill large orders, and manage logistics more efficiently.

* Admins:
Platform managers who oversee users, orders, payments, and supply chain activity.

Merchant Module

I designed the merchant dashboard to give buyers a clear view of their order activities, orders by country, and average delivery time. Merchants can browse by product type, filter by country, and manage ongoing orders from a single, easy-to-navigate interface.

Supplier Module

Suppliers can upload products, manage pricing, and track incoming orders. I structured this app to prioritize efficiency—making it easy for vendors to respond to merchant requests, update product details, and process bulk sales.

Outcome

Cazuku came together just the way we imagined it—a simple, functional eCommerce platform where merchants can find trusted suppliers, place bulk orders, and manage everything from one place. The experience feels smooth on both ends, and the system is built in a way that can grow easily as more users come on board.

What I learned

This project taught me a lot about designing for different user types at once. I had to think about how to make things easy for both suppliers and merchants without one side feeling more important. It also helped me get better at structuring dashboards and breaking down big processes (like logistics and bulk orders) into simple, usable flows.

EFCC Case Management Portal

Type

Web App (SaaS)

Date

2024

Role

UX/UI Design Lead

Client

Vatebra Ltd

The application is a self-service automated Legal Case Management System designed to streamline the logging, assignment, tracking, and reporting of all cases handled by the Economic Financial Crimes Commission, resolving manual reporting challenges and providing executives with a holistic view of case statuses.

The challenge

Before now, case management at EFCC involved a lot of manual work—things like logging, assigning, tracking, and reporting cases were done across disconnected systems. This made it hard to follow up on case progress, caused reporting delays, and gave top-level executives very little visibility into what was happening in real time.

The solution

We designed a self-service Legal Case Management System that takes away all the manual stress. From logging a case to assigning it, tracking progress, and reporting outcomes—everything now happens in one central platform. It also gives EFCC leadership a clear view of all ongoing cases, making decision-making faster and more informed.
